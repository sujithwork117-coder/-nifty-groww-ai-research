# Ekalayava Post-entry Behavior Across Two Periods

**Scope:** saved Ekalayava entry/event outputs and already downloaded 5-minute option candles, Period A (2026-04-25–2026-09-25) and independent Period B (2026-01-01–2026-04-24). Actual baseline sessions: A 106 (2026-04-27 through 2026-09-25); B 76 (2026-01-01 through 2026-04-24). This is descriptive behavior analysis. No market data was downloaded, no baseline rerun, no strategy code/rules changed, and no exit rule added.

## Method and boundaries

- Saved event sources: `data/reports/baseline_v1_requested_period.json` and `data/reports/baseline_v1_period_b_2026-01-01_to_2026-04-24.json`; OHLC from the corresponding existing selected contract files in `data/derived/requested_period_2026-04-25_2026-09-25/options/` and `data/derived/period_b_2026-01-01_to_2026-04-24/options/`. There are 170 A and 132 B events (302 total); every entry candle exists.
- Entry, target, swing reference, breakdown timestamp, opening levels, and saved MFE/MAE come from baseline events. Cross-checking event MFE/MAE against the cached candle path found **0 mismatches** in both periods.
- The existing simulator examines later candles on the same IST date only (up to 78 rows), stops at the first bar with high >= Opening High, and otherwise records OPEN at the observed same-day horizon. It has no SL or other exit. OPEN is not an actual position carried to the end of the five-month period. TARGET is a target-touch observation, not complete realized P&L.
- MFE/MAE and timing use premium points/minutes on that simulator path, censored at target touch or the same-day horizon. Target times are conditional on observed target touches.
- The pre-entry “sequence trough” is the minimum observed premium low from the first Opening-Low-break bar through the bar before entry. It is a causal descriptive proxy only: the canonical rules do not define a swing-low. The swing reference is the implementation’s causal swing high. Breakout H/L are entry-candle values; breakout close equals the entry price. No future bars define these levels.
- Low “revisit” means a later observed candle low <= the reference. The path ends at target when target occurs; post-target behavior is not assessed. A bar with both high above and low below entry does not reveal intrabar ordering.

## 5. Cross-period observations

### Seen in both observed samples

- Adverse lows appeared soon after entry: mean first-adverse time was 5.9 min A and 5.3 min B. Some observed low below entry occurred in 170/170 A events and 129/132 B events.
- First-bar OHLC often spans both sides of entry, preventing order determination (91.2% A, 93.2% B).
- Structural-low retest frequencies are similar across these samples: ~58% sequence trough, ~78% breakdown low, and ~73–75% breakout low.
- Entry hours 09:00 and 10:00 account for 123/170 (72.4%) A and 98/132 (74.2%) B entries.
- Both samples have TARGET and OPEN outcomes under the same target-only lifecycle, so neither produces full Ekalayava P&L.

### Differences observed

- B mean/median MFE was 42.84/29.35 vs A 36.76/23.73 premium points. B mean MAE was more negative (-47.91 vs -43.95), while median MAE was similar (-36.80 vs -36.08).
- Observed target-touch fractions were 58/132 (43.9%) B and 82/170 (48.2%) A. Among target rows, mean/median time-to-target was 73.9/45 min B vs 106.0/85 min A. Missing bars can hide earlier touches or excursions and affect observed timing.
- Hour composition differs: 48 A and 40 B entries at 09:00; 75 A and 58 B at 10:00. Observed target fractions were 60.4% vs 70.0% at 09:00, and 44.0% vs 32.8% at 10:00, respectively. Small late-hour groups should not be generalized.
- CE target fraction exceeded PE in A (53.2% vs 44.0%); in B CE/PE fractions were closer (43.1%/45.0%). ITM3/ITM2 target fractions were 49.4%/47.1% A and 46.0%/42.0% B. These are descriptive subgroup counts only.

## 7. Lifecycle limitation

The canonical strategy specifies its entry sequence and Opening High target, but not an SL, opposite-signal exit, forced end-of-day close, expiry handling, or end-of-data valuation. The target-only simulator labels events without a same-day target as OPEN. Period A’s 3,574.6 target-point sum and Period B target points are target-touch subset values, not complete or realized P&L. See `reports/ekalayava_spec_implementation_audit.md`.
